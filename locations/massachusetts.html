Massachusetts Vocational Evaluation Services

Massachusetts Workers' Compensation

Expert vocational evaluations for Massachusetts workers' compensation cases under M.G.L. Chapter 152. Our assessments address:

  • Suitable alternative employment analysis
  • Earning capacity assessments per DIA guidelines
  • Vocational rehabilitation planning
  • Section 35 and 36 evaluations
  • Testimony before the Department of Industrial Accidents

Social Security Disability (SSDI/SSI)

Comprehensive vocational assessments for Social Security disability claims in Massachusetts:

  • Residual Functional Capacity (RFC) analysis
  • Transferable skills assessments
  • Grid rule applications
  • Vocational expert testimony for ALJ hearings
  • Appeals Council and Federal Court cases

Personal Injury & Medical Malpractice

Expert vocational opinions for Massachusetts personal injury litigation:

  • Loss of earning capacity calculations
  • Future wage loss projections
  • Life care planning coordination
  • Vocational testing and assessment
  • Expert witness testimony in Superior Court

Long-Term Disability Insurance

Vocational evaluations for LTD claims with Massachusetts insurers:

  • "Own occupation" vs. "any occupation" analysis
  • Reasonable accommodation assessments
  • Vocational rehabilitation planning
  • Independent vocational evaluations
  • ERISA claim support
