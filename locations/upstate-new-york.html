Expert vocational evaluations for New York workers' compensation cases under WCL Articles 1-13. Our assessments address:

  • Labor market attachment analysis
  • Wage earning capacity assessments per WCB guidelines
  • Vocational rehabilitation under Section 8
  • Schedule Loss of Use evaluations
  • Testimony before Workers' Compensation Board
  • Independent Medical Examination (IME) coordination
