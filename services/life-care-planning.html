Vocational Support for Life Care Planning

Life care planning requires a multidisciplinary approach to address the comprehensive needs of individuals with catastrophic injuries or chronic disabilities. As vocational rehabilitation experts, we provide critical input on the vocational and functional aspects of life care plans, helping to ensure that individuals receive appropriate support for maximum independence and quality of life.

Our vocational expertise complements the work of life care planners, nurses, and physicians by addressing work capacity, vocational rehabilitation needs, attendant care requirements, and the functional implications of disability. We serve clients throughout Massachusetts and Upstate New York, providing thorough assessments that support comprehensive life care planning.

Our Role in Life Care Planning

1. Functional Capacity Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of physical and cognitive abilities to determine support needs for daily living and potential vocational activities.

2. Vocational Potential Analysis

Assessment of current and future work capacity, including supported employment options and vocational rehabilitation needs.

3. Attendant Care Evaluation

Analysis of personal care assistance needs based on functional limitations and independence goals.

4. Activity & Participation Assessment

Evaluation of ability to engage in educational, vocational, recreational, and community activities with appropriate support.

5. Assistive Technology Needs

Identification of adaptive equipment and technology to maximize independence in daily living and vocational pursuits.

6. Long-Term Support Planning

Projection of changing support needs over the lifespan, including transitions in care and vocational goals.

Areas of Expertise

Traumatic Brain Injury

Vocational implications of cognitive impairment, supported employment needs, and long-term supervision requirements.

Spinal Cord Injury

Functional capacity assessment, wheelchair accessibility needs, and vocational accommodation planning.

Amputation

Prosthetic training needs, vocational modifications, and long-term equipment replacement planning.

Chronic Pain Conditions

Activity tolerance assessment, pacing strategies, and vocational feasibility with pain management.

Multiple Trauma

Complex functional assessment addressing multiple body systems and comprehensive support needs.

Pediatric Disabilities

Educational and pre-vocational planning, transition services, and long-term vocational potential.

Key Components of Our Assessment

  • Activities of Daily Living: Detailed analysis of self-care abilities and support needs
  • Instrumental Activities: Assessment of community living skills including shopping, transportation, and household management
  • Educational Needs: Evaluation of learning capacity and educational support requirements
  • Vocational Feasibility: Analysis of work potential including supported employment options
  • Care Coordination: Assessment of case management and care coordination needs
  • Equipment & Modifications: Identification of durable medical equipment and home/vehicle modifications
  • Attendant Care Hours: Quantification of personal care assistance needs by time of day
