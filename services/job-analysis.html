Job Analysis & Placement Services

Our job analysis and placement services bridge the gap between an individual's capabilities and suitable employment opportunities. We provide detailed assessments of job requirements, identify appropriate accommodations, and facilitate successful return-to-work outcomes for individuals with disabilities or injuries throughout Massachusetts and Upstate New York.

With extensive experience in vocational rehabilitation and deep knowledge of regional labor markets, we help individuals find sustainable employment that matches their skills, interests, and functional abilities while meeting employer needs. Our services benefit workers' compensation cases, disability insurance claims, and individuals seeking to re-enter the workforce after injury or illness.

Our Job Analysis Process

1. Functional Capacity Review

We analyze medical documentation and functional capacity evaluations to understand the individual's physical and cognitive abilities and limitations.

2. Skills Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of education, work history, transferable skills, and vocational interests to identify suitable occupation targets.

3. Job Market Analysis

Research of available positions in the local labor market that match the individual's capabilities and career objectives.

4. On-Site Job Analysis

When appropriate, we conduct workplace evaluations to document physical demands, environmental conditions, and essential job functions.

5. Accommodation Planning

Identification of reasonable accommodations, assistive technology, and workplace modifications that enable successful job performance.

6. Placement Support

Active assistance with job applications, interview preparation, and employer negotiations to facilitate successful placement.

Key Components of Job Analysis

  • Physical Demands Analysis: Documentation of lifting, carrying, standing, sitting, and other physical requirements
  • Cognitive Requirements: Assessment of mental demands including decision-making, concentration, and memory needs
  • Environmental Factors: Evaluation of workplace conditions including noise, temperature, and exposure elements
  • Essential Functions: Identification of core job duties that must be performed with or without accommodation
  • Marginal Functions: Documentation of non-essential tasks that could be reassigned or eliminated
  • Equipment & Tools: Analysis of machinery, computers, and tools required for job performance
  • Schedule & Flexibility: Review of work hours, breaks, and scheduling accommodation possibilities
