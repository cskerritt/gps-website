Vocational Expert Witness Services

Timothy R. Andenmatten provides expert witness testimony in state and federal courts throughout Massachusetts and Upstate New York. With 19+ years of experience and credentials as a Certified Rehabilitation Counselor (CRC) and Certified Vocational Evaluation Specialist (CVE), he delivers authoritative, well-supported testimony that helps attorneys and courts understand complex vocational issues.

Our expert witness services combine deep vocational expertise with exceptional communication skills, ensuring that judges and juries clearly understand the vocational implications of disability, injury, and employment disputes. We provide testimony that is credible, defensible, and based on accepted methodologies in the field of vocational rehabilitation.

Areas of Expert Testimony

Disability Insurance Litigation

Own occupation and any occupation analyses for short-term and long-term disability claims. Expert opinions on vocational capacity, transferable skills, and reasonable accommodations.

Workers' Compensation

Testimony regarding permanent disability ratings, vocational rehabilitation needs, return-to-work feasibility, and labor market access following workplace injuries.

Personal Injury Cases

Expert opinions on earning capacity loss, vocational impact of injuries, future employment prospects, and lifetime economic damages.

Employment Law

Testimony in wrongful termination, discrimination, and failure to accommodate cases. Analysis of reasonable accommodations and essential job functions.

Social Security Disability

Expert testimony for administrative hearings and federal court appeals regarding residual functional capacity and vocational factors.

Family Law

Earning capacity evaluations and testimony in divorce proceedings, particularly regarding imputed income and vocational potential.
