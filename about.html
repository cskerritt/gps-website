About Greylock Placement Services

Leading vocational experts dedicated to comprehensive evaluations and professional testimony

Our Mission

Greylock Placement Services LLC (G-P-S) is committed to providing the highest quality vocational evaluation services to legal professionals, insurance companies, and individuals navigating disability and injury claims. We bridge the gap between medical limitations and vocational possibilities, offering expert analysis that stands up to scrutiny in legal proceedings.

Our Approach

We believe in comprehensive, evidence-based evaluations that consider the whole person. Our assessments integrate medical information, educational background, work history, and current labor market conditions to provide accurate, defensible opinions about vocational potential and earning capacity.

Leadership

Timothy R. Andenmatten, MEd, CRC, CVE, IPEC

Founder & Lead Vocational Expert

Timothy R. Andenmatten brings over 19 years of specialized experience in vocational rehabilitation counseling to Greylock Placement Services. As a Certified Rehabilitation Counselor (CRC) and Certified Vocational Evaluation Specialist (CVE), he has established himself as a leading expert in disability evaluation and vocational assessment.

His extensive background includes positions with major insurance carriers including Sun Life, MetLife, and Unum, where he specialized in Long-Term Disability (LTD) and Individual Disability Insurance (IDI) evaluations. Timothy's expertise encompasses Own Occupation and Transferable Skills Analyses, vocational definitions of disability, appeals processes, return-to-work services, and ADA accommodations.

As a U.S. Social Security Administration Vocational Expert, Timothy frequently provides testimony before Administrative Law Judges and supervises a team of over 10 contracted vocational experts. His comprehensive understanding of industry standards and insurer expectations, combined with his commitment to objective, evidence-based evaluations, makes him a trusted expert for attorneys, insurance companies, and government agencies nationwide.
