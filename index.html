Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vocational evaluation?

A vocational evaluation is a comprehensive assessment process that analyzes an individual's work-related abilities, limitations, and potential for employment following an injury or disability.

When is a vocational expert needed?

Vocational experts are typically needed in personal injury cases, workers' compensation claims, disability determinations, and any legal matter involving questions about work capacity or earning potential.

What documents are needed for an evaluation?

Typically required documents include medical records, employment history, educational records, tax returns, and any relevant legal documents related to the case.

How long does an evaluation take?

A comprehensive vocational evaluation typically takes 2-4 weeks from initial consultation to final report, depending on case complexity and document availability.
